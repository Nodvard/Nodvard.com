Hosting on GitHub Pages

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open the site, GitHub processes the data needed to deliver it. This includes your IP address, date and time, the requested address, your browser’s user agent and the page you came from. GitHub keeps this data in server logs to run the service securely.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a reachable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA. Read more in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Domain and email at Cloudflare

The domain nodvard.com is managed at Cloudflare, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Requests to this site pass through Cloudflare’s servers before they reach GitHub. Cloudflare processes the same technical data as GitHub, mainly your IP address, to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. Cloudflare sets no cookies here. Emails to addresses at @nodvard.com are received by Cloudflare and forwarded to a mailbox at Google (Gmail).

Here too, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in a reachable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for details.

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. There are no statistics, no ads and no embedded content. Fonts and images come from the same server as the site, nothing is loaded from third parties. That is why there is no cookie banner.

To keep spam bots from reading the email address on this site, Cloudflare encodes it when delivering the page. A small Cloudflare script, also loaded from nodvard.com, makes it readable again in your browser. It sets no cookies and collects no data. Without JavaScript, you see a notice instead of the address.

Links to other sites

The site links to Discord, GitHub and Cloudflare. You only leave nodvard.com when you click one of these links. The privacy rules of that provider apply there.

Email

If you send us an email, we only use your details to reply. Afterwards we delete them, unless we are required to keep them.
