Privacy

In short: this site sets no cookies, uses no tracking and loads nothing from other servers. Only the usual server logs are kept by the host.

Draft, needs a legal review. This text describes what technically happens on the site. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer yet. The German version is the one that applies.

Hosting on GitHub Pages

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open the site, GitHub processes the data needed to deliver it. This includes your IP address, date and time, the requested address, your browser’s user agent and the page you came from. GitHub keeps this data in server logs to run the service securely.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a reachable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA. Read more in GitHub’s privacy statement.

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. There are no statistics, no ads and no embedded content. The site uses your device’s fonts, and images come from the same server as the site. That is why there is no cookie banner.

Links to other sites

The site links to Discord and GitHub. You only leave nodvard.com when you click one of these links. The privacy rules of that provider apply there.

Email

If you send us an email, we only use your details to reply. Afterwards we delete them, unless we are required to keep them.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correction, deletion and restriction of processing, to data portability and to object. You can also complain to a data protection authority. Write to kontakt@nodvard.com.

The Nodvard Deck program

This policy only covers the website nodvard.com. You run the Nodvard Deck program yourself, on your own machine.
