Hosting on GitHub Pages

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open the site, GitHub processes the data needed to deliver it. This includes your IP address, date and time, the requested address, your browser’s user agent and the page you came from. GitHub keeps this data in server logs to run the service securely. According to GitHub, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes when a GitHub Pages site is visited (GitHub Pages, data collection). GitHub does not state a fixed retention period for this.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a reachable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA. By its own account, GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (GitHub’s privacy statement), so the European Commission’s adequacy decision applies to the transfer (Art. 45 GDPR). Read more in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Domain and email at Cloudflare

The domain nodvard.com is managed at Cloudflare, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Requests to this site pass through Cloudflare’s servers before they reach GitHub. Cloudflare processes the same technical data as GitHub, mainly your IP address, to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. Cloudflare sets no cookies here. Cloudflare’s privacy policy does not state a fixed retention period for this data.

Here too, the legal basis is my legitimate interest in a reachable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA. By its own account, Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Cloudflare’s privacy policy), so the European Commission’s adequacy decision applies to the transfer (Art. 45 GDPR). See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for details.

Email forwarding

Emails to addresses at @nodvard.com are received by Cloudflare’s Email Routing service and forwarded to a mailbox at Google (Gmail). Cloudflare processes sender, recipient and content of the email to deliver it According to Cloudflare, Email Routing does not store or access the forwarded emails (Cloudflare Email Routing). For users in the EEA, the controller for the mailbox is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (Google’s privacy policy). Data may be transferred to the USA here as well. By its own account, Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Google’s data transfer frameworks).

The legal basis for forwarding is my legitimate interest in reliably receiving messages to @nodvard.com in one place (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If your email is about a contract or a request for one, such as a license for a company, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. There are no statistics, no ads and no embedded content. Fonts and images come from the same server as the site, nothing is loaded from third parties. That is why there is no cookie banner.

To keep spam bots from reading the email address, Cloudflare encodes it on most pages when delivering them. In the imprint and in this privacy policy it is in plain text on purpose, because it must always be readable there. A small Cloudflare script, also loaded from nodvard.com, makes it readable again in your browser. It sets no cookies and collects no data. Without JavaScript, you see a notice instead of the address.

Links to other sites

The site links to Discord, GitHub and Cloudflare. You only leave nodvard.com when you click one of these links. The privacy rules of that provider apply there.
